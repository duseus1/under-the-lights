# Under the Lights v1.1.0

Your career has a memory now. This update brings the little details that make a season feel like yours, for both Madden and NBA 2K.

[Download v1.1.0 for Windows](Under%20the%20Lights_1.1.0_x64-setup.exe)

- **Career snapshot:** your player’s résumé, role, production, awards, relationships, and story chapter.
- **Recent form:** last-five trends and authored conversations that recognize hot streaks, slumps, improvement, and inconsistency.
- **Career tendencies:** choices build a remembered pattern without adding buffs or a morality meter.
- **Previously…:** a short reminder of the decisions and results leading into major story events.
- **Remembered moments:** bookmark games, conversations, and records, and give games your own titles and notes.
- **Correction previews:** see what a box-score edit will change before confirming it.
- **Undo last check-in:** restore one complete game or conversation checkpoint, including its rewards and consequences. Another career change closes the undo window.
- **Franchise and rookie records:** enter your team’s baselines and trigger historic conversations without needing to break a league record.

Existing careers remain compatible. All dialogue and rules run locally, with no AI service required. The rookie-season scope is unchanged.

Validation: 343 unit tests, 17 browser journeys, and native Windows SQLite checks passed. See `SHA256SUMS.txt` for installer checksums.
